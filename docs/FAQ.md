# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General

**Q: Do I need an internet connection to study?**
**A:** No! Formula Flash is built with an "Offline First" philosophy. Once you have created or imported your sets, all your data and the computer algebra system run completely locally on your device. You can study on an airplane or in a subway with zero issues.

**Q: Where is my data stored?**
**A:** Your flashcards and study progress are stored locally on your device using IndexedDB. If you clear your browser or app data, you will lose your sets unless you have exported them. We highly recommend regularly exporting your important sets to `.flashset` files as backups.

## Studying & Inputs

**Q: Does it grade math accurately? What if I write $x+y$ and the answer is $y+x$?**
**A:** Formula Flash uses a powerful computer algebra system (`@cortex-js/compute-engine`). It evaluates answers algebraically. As long as your expression is mathematically equivalent to the correct answer, it will be marked correct. So $x+y$ is recognized as exactly the same as $y+x$.

**Q: Are there keyboard shortcuts when studying on a computer?**
**A:** Yes!
- **Enter:** Submit answer / Continue to next card.
- **Space:** Flip card (in Flashcard mode).
- **Numbers 1-4:** Rate how well you knew the answer (in Flashcard mode).
- **Arrow Keys:** Navigate through standard UI elements.

## Mobile and Android

**Q: How do I install it on Android?**
**A:** Go to the [Releases page](https://github.com/DontKwo/math-flashcards/releases) on GitHub from your Android phone and download the latest `formulaflash-debug.apk`. Tap the downloaded file to install it. You may need to grant permission to "Install unknown apps" in your Android settings.

**Q: Will it be on the Google Play Store or Apple App Store?**
**A:** Currently, Formula Flash is provided as a direct APK download for Android, and as a web app. You can also "Install" the web app to your home screen on iOS via Safari (Add to Home Screen) as a Progressive Web App (PWA).

## Troubleshooting

**Q: The math keyboard isn't showing up when I tap a field.**
**A:** Ensure you are tapping on a designated math field. Standard text fields will only bring up your device's default keyboard. If you are on a desktop/laptop, the on-screen math keyboard is usually hidden in favor of your physical keyboard.

**Q: I imported a `.flashset` file, but some cards look broken.**
**A:** Ensure the `.flashset` file was generated by a compatible version of Formula Flash. If you manually edited the file, ensure the JSON structure and Zod schema validation rules were not violated.
